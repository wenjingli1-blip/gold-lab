Principal Investigator

RJ

Rory Johnson

Principal Investigator

rory.johnson@ucd.ie

Rory Johnson leads the GOLD Lab at University College Dublin, where he holds the position of Associate Professor. He is also an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the University of Bern and previously served as a Junior Group Leader at the Swiss National Centre for Competence in Research in RNA & Disease (NCCR RNA & Disease, 2016–2021).
