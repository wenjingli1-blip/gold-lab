New Publication May 3, 2024

New Review: Long noncoding RNAs in Oncology

Michela Coan has published a landmark review article on long noncoding RNAs in oncology in Nature Reviews Genetics. The review covers lncRNA classification, mechanisms of dysregulation in tumours, and the emerging landscape of RNA-targeting therapeutics.
