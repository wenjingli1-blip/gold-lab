Our Culture

What we stand for

We believe that the strength of our science arises from the diversity of our ideas and experiences. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, collaborative laboratory culture.

Goal-centred science

We do not draw a rigid line between experimental and computational work. Our projects use all tools critical for success — and when tools don't exist, we build them.

Diverse & inclusive

We welcome students, postdocs, and visiting scholars from all backgrounds, nationalities, and disciplines. Diversity of perspective drives better science.

International network

Our collaborations span Lausanne, Zurich, London, Barcelona, Singapore, and beyond. Lab members are exposed to a wide international scientific community.

Open & sustainable science

All our tools are open-source. We are proud holders of a Green Lab Certification, reflecting our commitment to sustainable laboratory practice.

What We Offer

World-class research environment

Access to state-of-the-art genomics facilities at UCD and through our partner institutions. Full access to high-performance computing clusters for bioinformatic analysis.

Training across disciplines

Whether your background is in wet-lab biology, computational science, or engineering, you will receive training in both experimental and computational approaches to RNA biology.

International conference travel

Lab members are supported to present their work at leading international conferences in genomics, RNA biology, and cancer biology, including EMBO, CSHL, and NCCR events.

Career development

Regular one-to-one mentoring with Rory Johnson, structured feedback on manuscripts and grant applications, and opportunities for cross-disciplinary collaboration and co-supervision.

Competitive funding

Our lab is funded by the Irish Research Council, SFI, HRB, MSCA, and Chinese Scholarship Council. Positions include competitive stipends and Irish university fees.

Life in Dublin

UCD is located in Belfield, Dublin — one of Europe's most vibrant, English-speaking capital cities. Ireland's thriving biosciences ecosystem offers strong career prospects beyond academia.

Get in touch

Application Process

How to apply

All applications begin with a direct email to the PI. We are informal and responsive — there is no online portal or form.

  1. 1

    Read our research

    Browse the Research and Publications pages to understand our current projects. Identify which area aligns with your background and interests.

  2. 2

    Email Rory directly

    Send an email to rory.johnson@ucd.ie with the subject line matching your intended position. Include:

    • —A brief cover letter (1 page max) explaining your interest and background
    • —Your CV / résumé including academic record and publications
    • —Contact details for 2–3 references (referees will only be contacted later)
  3. 3

    Initial conversation

    Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an informal video call with Rory to discuss the science, the project, and mutual expectations. No formal interview panel at this stage.

  4. 4

    Visit the lab

    Promising candidates are invited to spend a day in the lab, meet the team, and give a short presentation on their previous work. Travel support is provided where possible.
