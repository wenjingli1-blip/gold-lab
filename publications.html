Our Work

Publications

A record of our scientific contributions — from foundational lncRNA biology to clinical diagnostics and open-source genomics tools. Full lists available on Google Scholar ↗ and PubMed ↗.

2024

Long noncoding RNAs in oncology

Coan M, Johnson R. Nature Reviews Genetics (2024)

A comprehensive review of lncRNA function, dysregulation, and therapeutic potential in human cancer, covering classification, mechanisms, and clinical translation.

Review Cancer lncRNA Biology
May 2024 View article ↗

TransCistor: efficient cis-regulatory lncRNA identification

Johnson R et al. Bioinformatics (2024)

TransCistor is a computational tool for the large-scale identification of lncRNAs that act in cis to regulate neighbouring protein-coding genes, enabling systematic functional annotation.

Tool lncRNA Biology
Mar 2024 View article ↗

2023

CASPR: end-to-end CRISPR screen analysis for single and paired RNA-guides

Pulido-Quetglas C, Johnson R et al. Genome Biology (2023)

CASPR provides comprehensive analysis of CRISPR screens targeting lncRNAs, supporting both single and paired sgRNA designs from raw sequencing to ranked hit lists.

Tool CRISPR
2023 View article ↗

Evolutionary conservation reveals functional lncRNAs in the human genome

Johnson R, Guigó R et al. Nature Communications (2023)

Cross-species conservation analysis identifies a functionally constrained subset of human lncRNAs, with implications for annotation quality and disease relevance.

lncRNA Biology Annotation
2023 View article ↗

2019

Transposable elements mediate specific subcellular localisation of long noncoding RNAs in human cells

Carlevaro-Fita J, Johnson R et al. Genome Research (2019)

Identified a role for evolutionarily-conserved L2b, MIRb and MIRc repetitive elements in directing lncRNAs to nuclear vs cytoplasmic compartments in human cells.

lncRNA Biology Transposable Elements
2019 View article ↗

2017

ExInAtor: a pipeline for the discovery of cancer driver lncRNAs using somatic mutations

Lanzós A, Johnson R et al. Scientific Reports (2017)

ExInAtor identifies lncRNA driver candidates in whole cancer genomes by testing for elevated mutation rates in lncRNA exons against a local background model, applied to the PCAWG cohort.

Tool Cancer PCAWG
2017 View article ↗

CRISPETa: a tool for the design of efficient paired CRISPR-Cas9 deletion experiments

Pulido-Quetglas C, Johnson R et al. PLOS Computational Biology (2017)

CRISPETa automates the design of paired sgRNAs for CRISPR excision of genomic elements, accessible to bench and computational biologists. Scales from single genes to genome-wide screens.

Tool CRISPR
2017 View article ↗

2015

DECKO: a simple and scalable vector system for CRISPR-based deletion experiments

Aparicio-Prat E, Johnson R et al. BMC Genomics (2015)

Describes the DECKO (Dual Excision CRISPR Knockout) lentiviral vector system for delivering paired sgRNAs, enabling efficient deletion of lncRNA loci from single genes to genome-wide scales.

Tool CRISPR
2015 View article ↗

2014

The RIDL hypothesis: transposable elements as functional domains of long noncoding RNAs

Johnson R, Guigó R. RNA (2014)

Proposes that neofunctionalised transposable element fragments (RIDLs) serve as sequence domains encoding lncRNA biological activity, establishing a new paradigm for understanding lncRNA function.

lncRNA Biology Transposable Elements Foundational
2014 View article ↗

Earlier Work

The GENCODE v7 catalogue of human long noncoding RNAs: analysis of their gene structure, evolution, and expression

Derrien T*, Johnson R*, Bussotti G et al. Genome Research (2012) *equal contribution

The foundational GENCODE lncRNA annotation paper. Characterises 9,277 manually annotated lncRNA genes across 15,512 transcripts, providing the first comprehensive view of human lncRNA gene structure, conservation, and expression patterns.

Annotation lncRNA Biology Foundational
2012 View article ↗

Genome-wide inference of natural selection on human transcription factor binding sites

Johnson R, Wegner S, Bhutani N et al. Nature Genetics (2007)

Early work from Rory Johnson's PhD and postdoctoral training period, applying population-genomic methods to identify regulatory elements under purifying natural selection across the human genome.

Genomics Evolution
