Our Mission

Better therapies through the science of RNA

We are an interdisciplinary, international research group based at University College Dublin. Our mission is to develop better therapies for common diseases through understanding RNA — the original molecule of Life. We use CRISPR-Cas genome-editing and bioinformatics to discover and drug disease-causing RNAs, with a particular focus on the important yet mysterious class of long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs).

We do not draw a rigid line between experimental and computational work. Our projects are goal-centred and draw on every tool critical for success — and when existing tools fall short, we build new ones.
